Reading a series should not mean updating the same chapter in several places. With reading trackers, KMServer can keep your AniList, MyAnimeList, Bangumi, and MangaBaka entries up to date as you finish books.

## Connect your accounts

Open **Trackers** from the account menu and sign in to the platforms you use. Each KMServer user connects their own accounts, so everyone keeps progress in their own profiles. You can disconnect a platform at any time.

## Link once, then keep reading

Open a series and link it to the matching entry on a tracker. After that, finishing a book updates your progress automatically — no extra step between reading and logging.

**Auto** works out whether to track chapters or volumes from the book names in the series. If a series needs a different convention, choose **Chapter** or **Volume** explicitly. A chapter offset is also available for series whose remote numbering starts somewhere different.

## Progress moves forward, not backward

KMServer looks at the completed books in the series and only moves tracker progress forward. Marking a book unread or removing its progress does not lower the value on the tracker.

Finishing the last known chapter or volume can complete the remote entry. Starting an entry records its start date, and advancing a completed entry can switch it to rereading when the platform supports that state.

Synchronization is one-way: progress is sent to the tracker, not pulled back into KMServer. A failed update does not interrupt your reading.

## You stay in control

Each linked entry also has manual controls for status, score, chapter, and volume. You can choose which libraries show tracker actions and set a default platform for new links.

Open **Trackers** to connect an account, then link a series from its detail page. The [feature guide](/server/enhancements/trackers/) has the details.
