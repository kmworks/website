KMReader keeps every Komga login, preference, and cache on your device. There is no KMReader account, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

## What stays on your device

KMReader stores the following locally so you can use several servers, load quickly, and keep reading offline:

- Komga server addresses, display names, and sign-in credentials — an app-managed API key when the server supports one, otherwise your username and password
- Reading progress, cached pages and covers, and downloaded books
- Preferences like themes, layouts, and cache budgets

We cannot view or retrieve any of it. KMReader does not use iCloud sync, analytics, or crash reporting.

## How local storage works

Everything lives in KMReader's app sandbox, which iOS and macOS encrypt at rest as part of standard data protection. Removing a server profile deletes its saved credentials, cached pages, covers, and downloaded books.

You control when to clear caches or sign out. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

## Network connections

- **Your Komga servers.** KMReader talks directly to the servers you add, using HTTPS when your server supports it. We never proxy, log, or inspect that traffic.
- **Manga translation model packs.** When you download an OCR model pack, KMReader fetches the catalog and the pack from `models.kmworks.date`. These are plain file downloads: no account, library content, or reading data is sent, though the host sees the usual request details such as your IP address.
- **Your translation provider**, if you configure one (see below).
- **Apple services** you choose to use, such as in-app purchases through the App Store, Handoff, Spotlight, and widgets, are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

## Manga translation

Text detection and OCR run entirely on your device — page images are never uploaded, to us or to anyone else.

If you use Apple's built-in translation, the recognized text stays on your device too. If you configure a custom AI translation provider, only the recognized text is sent, over your own API key, to the endpoint you chose. With **Include Comic Metadata** turned on (it is off by default), the series and chapter titles, authors, and genres are added to that request. That traffic goes straight from your device to your provider; we run no servers in between.

## No third-party tracking

The app ships without advertising SDKs, analytics beacons, social trackers, or data brokers. What happens in KMReader stays with you.

## Managing or deleting data

Because everything lives on-device, you can:

- Remove a server profile to delete its credentials, caches, and downloads
- Use the in-app cache controls to clear pages, books, or covers
- Delete the app to remove every remaining file and preference

If you reinstall KMReader, you start with a clean slate.
